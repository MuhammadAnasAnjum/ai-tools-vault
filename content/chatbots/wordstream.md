# WordStream in 2026: The Software Is Gone, the Free Tools Remain

If you are researching WordStream because a listicle recommended it as PPC management software, stop and read this first. **The WordStream Advisor platform was discontinued.** Subscriptions were wound down and existing customers lost access around the April 2022 AdWords API deprecation, with competitors publishing migration guides for stranded users.

What survives is real, useful, and much smaller than the marketing archive suggests. Here is the accurate picture.

## What is WordStream now?

WordStream was founded as a PPC management platform, acquired by Gannett in 2018, and folded into **LOCALiQ**, Gannett's marketing services arm within the USA TODAY NETWORK. In December 2021 the brand relaunched as "WordStream by LocaliQ," and the self-serve software was retired.

Today wordstream.com operates as a **content and free-tools property**. Three tools work and are worth using: the **Google Ads Performance Grader**, the **Facebook Ads Performance Grader**, and the **Free Keyword Tool**. The marketing blog remains active and is a legitimately good PPC education resource.

Paid campaign work is sold as **LOCALiQ managed services** through a sales consultation. There is no self-serve WordStream pricing page and no subscription product to buy.

One warning worth stating directly: several pages dated 2026 claim WordStream costs "$49/month and up" and describe a live "20-Minute Work Week" dashboard. These are competitor and affiliate pages recycling pre-2022 descriptions. No vendor source supports current Advisor pricing, because the product does not exist.

## Key Features

- **Google Ads Performance Grader** — audits a connected account and scores it against benchmarks drawn from more than 16,000 campaigns across 23 industries, with a 30-day performance tracker.
- **Facebook Ads Performance Grader** — the equivalent audit for Meta advertising.
- **Free Keyword Tool** — keyword research with volume and competition estimates, filterable by industry and country.
- **Benchmark data** — the comparison against a large real-campaign dataset is the genuinely differentiated part.
- **Active blog and learning resources** — consistently practical PPC guidance, updated regularly.
- **No account required for the keyword tool**, while the graders need Google or Meta account access to audit.
- **Web only** — no mobile app, no public API, no dashboard product.
- **Automated scoring rather than generative AI** — the analysis is benchmark comparison, not an AI assistant.

## Pros and Cons

**What is genuinely worth your time**

The Performance Grader remains a good free diagnostic. Connecting an account and getting a benchmarked score against a large industry dataset within minutes has real value, especially for a business that has never had its advertising audited. For a small business owner who suspects their agency is underperforming, or a marketer inheriting an account, it is a reasonable first look and it costs nothing.

The blog is better than most PPC content marketing and worth reading independently of the tools. The keyword tool is serviceable for quick research, though it will not replace a dedicated platform.

**What you need to know before using it**

Running the Grader makes you a **LOCALiQ sales lead**, and follow-up is persistent. That is the business model — the free audit exists to generate consultations. Go in expecting contact, and use an email address you are comfortable giving to a sales team.

The audit is also shallower than it appears. It skips conversion tracking quality, search-term waste analysis, audience targeting, and whether your bid strategy actually fits your goals — which are precisely the areas where most accounts leak money. Treat the score as a conversation starter, not a diagnosis.

Most importantly, this is not an ongoing management platform. If you need software to run campaigns daily, WordStream cannot do that regardless of what older reviews claim. Optmyzr, Adalysis, and Opteo occupy that space now, alongside Google's own tooling.

## How to Use WordStream's Free Tools

1. **Set expectations first.** You are using free diagnostics from a company that sells managed services, not evaluating a software purchase.
2. **Use an email you do not mind giving to sales** before connecting any account.
3. **Run the Google Ads Performance Grader** on a live account with at least a few weeks of spend — it needs data to benchmark against.
4. **Read the score as a prompt, not a verdict.** A weak grade tells you where to investigate; it does not tell you what is wrong.
5. **Check the gaps it misses yourself** — pull your search terms report for wasted spend, verify conversion tracking is firing correctly, and confirm your bid strategy matches your actual objective.
6. **Use the Free Keyword Tool for quick research**, filtered by industry and country, but verify volumes against Google Keyword Planner before building a campaign on them.
7. **If you need ongoing management software, look elsewhere** — the tools here are audits, not a platform.

### FAQs

**Is WordStream still available in 2026?**
The website, blog, and free tools are live under LOCALiQ. The paid WordStream Advisor software platform was discontinued, with subscriptions wound down around the April 2022 AdWords API deprecation. So the brand exists and the free tools work, but there is no PPC management product to subscribe to.

**How much does WordStream cost?**
The free tools cost nothing. There is no self-serve subscription — paid campaign work is sold as LOCALiQ managed services priced through a sales consultation. If you encounter a page quoting "$49/month" for WordStream, it is recycling pre-2022 information; those pages are affiliate content rather than vendor sources.

**Is the Google Ads Performance Grader worth running?**
Yes, with realistic expectations. It is a free benchmarked score against a large campaign dataset and a reasonable starting point for someone who has never audited their account. It will not examine conversion tracking quality, search-term waste, or bid strategy fit, and it generates a sales follow-up. Useful as a first look, not as a substitute for a real audit.

**What should I use instead for PPC management?**
For ongoing optimisation and automation, Optmyzr, Adalysis, and Opteo are the established options. For keyword research, Google Keyword Planner, Semrush, and Ahrefs go considerably deeper. For businesses that would rather outsource entirely, LOCALiQ's managed services are the direct successor to what WordStream once sold as software.
