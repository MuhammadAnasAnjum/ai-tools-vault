# Framer Review: The Design-Led AI Website Builder

Framer is a no-code website builder with a designer's sensibility, wrapping a visual canvas around a content management system, forms, hosting, analytics and on-page editing. In 2026 it leans heavily on AI, letting you prompt layouts and even build interactive components without writing code. This review weighs what that offers against the trade-offs.

The most important trade-off is lock-in: Framer does not let you export your code, so your site lives on its hosting. We will cover the AI features, the pricing structure, the vendor lock-in complaint, and a naming muddle that trips people up, so you can judge whether Framer suits your project.

## What is Framer?

Framer is an independent company founded around 2014 in Amsterdam by Koen Bok, its CEO, and Jorn van Dijk, both formerly of Sofa, a studio acquired by Facebook in 2011. It maintains its Amsterdam base with a San Francisco presence and has not been acquired. The product began life as an interactive design and prototyping tool, then pivoted into a full website builder — a shift that is well documented and defines what Framer is today.

The business has grown into what its backers call a "double unicorn". In August 2025 it raised a 100 million dollar Series D at a 2 billion dollar valuation, led by Meritech and Atomico, and it claims more than 500,000 monthly active users and an annual revenue pace near 100 million dollars. Its flagship release, Framer 3.0, launched in August 2026, bringing AI agents onto the canvas to design, write, analyse and organise sites, and introducing AI credits alongside simplified, cheaper plans. That built on Framer I/O 2025, which first shipped the AI suite.

## Key Features

- **Wireframer** — turns a natural-language prompt into a responsive layout or wireframe as a structural starting point.
- **Workshop** — "vibe coding" of interactive and custom components inside the editor, reportedly powered by Claude since around September 2025.
- **Canvas AI agents** — Framer 3.0 agents that design, write, analyse and organise sites directly on the canvas.
- **CMS and forms** — a built-in content management system and forms for dynamic, data-driven pages.
- **Hosting and analytics** — sites are published on Framer's hosting with analytics and A/B testing included.
- **On-page editing** — edit content directly on the live page rather than in a separate back end.
- **AI utilities** — AI Translate, Generate Images and Auto Rename, plus a Framer MCP plugin.
- **AI on the Free plan** — the AI features are available even without a paid subscription.

## Pros and Cons

**What works well**

Framer's design heritage shows. The canvas gives you precise visual control that many template-driven builders lack, and the result can look bespoke rather than off-the-shelf. The AI suite lowers the starting effort: Wireframer sketches a responsive structure from a prompt, and Workshop lets you conjure interactive components without hand-coding, which is unusual in a no-code tool. Bundling hosting, a CMS, forms, analytics and A/B testing means fewer moving parts and no separate services to stitch together. That AI is available even on the Free plan is welcoming for anyone testing the waters before committing.

**Where it falls short**

The most consistent complaint is vendor lock-in. Framer offers no code export, so your site is tied to its hosting and you cannot take the build elsewhere — a real risk to weigh for anything long-term. Pricing is charged per site, and extra editor seats cost more on top, which reviewers say adds up quickly for agencies and freelancers managing several clients. The October 2025 pricing overhaul drew backlash for gutting the entry tier and pushing the Pro plan as the practical minimum. There are also reported limits around SEO and structured data, and caps on CMS content. Wireframer, for its part, produces a structural starting point rather than a publishable site, so expect to do real design work afterwards.

**A naming clarification worth reading**

People often confuse two different things. Framer Motion, the popular React animation library, was spun off and rebranded simply "Motion" around late 2024 into 2025; its npm package moved from framer-motion to motion, it became framework-agnostic, and it is maintained separately by Matt Perry, who left Framer. That library is a distinct product from Framer the website builder reviewed here. If you arrived looking for the animation package, you want Motion, not this. For the record, no lawsuits or data breaches were found against Framer; the criticisms above are user sentiment rather than legal matters.

## How to Use Framer

1. **Sign up for the Free plan** — create an account and explore the canvas before paying.
2. **Start with a prompt or template** — use Wireframer to generate a responsive layout, or begin from a template.
3. **Refine on the canvas** — adjust structure, spacing, colour and typography with the visual editor.
4. **Add dynamic content** — set up the CMS and forms for blogs, listings or contact capture.
5. **Build custom pieces with Workshop** — describe an interactive component and let the AI assemble it.
6. **Localise and illustrate** — apply AI Translate and Generate Images where useful.
7. **Check SEO settings** — review metadata and structured-data options, mindful of the reported limits.
8. **Publish and measure** — deploy on Framer hosting, then watch analytics and run A/B tests.

### FAQs

**Is Framer free?**
There is a Free plan, and the AI features work on it. Paid tiers are reported to run from a Basic plan around 10 dollars a month, through Pro around 30 dollars and Scale from about 100 dollars, to custom Enterprise, billed annually. Pricing is per site and extra seats cost more, so confirm current figures on the vendor page.

**Can I export my site's code from Framer?**
No. Framer does not offer code export, so your site is tied to its hosting. This vendor lock-in is the most common criticism and worth weighing before you commit to a long-term project.

**Is Framer the same as Framer Motion?**
No. Framer Motion, the React animation library, was rebranded "Motion" and is now maintained separately by Matt Perry as a framework-agnostic package. Framer the website builder is a different product from the same original company.

**Is there a "Mini" plan?**
No. Framer discontinued the older "Mini" and "Launch" plans in an October 2025 restructuring that reduced the line to around five tiers. The entry paid tier is now "Basic". Verify the current tiers on the vendor page.
