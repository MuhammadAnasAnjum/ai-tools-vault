# Character.AI in 2026: Roleplay, Companionship, and a Much Stricter Platform

Character.AI is the largest platform built not for productivity but for conversation itself — roleplay, storytelling, companionship, and practice talking to someone. Users have created over 16 million characters, and engagement times have consistently outstripped those of general assistants.

It is also the AI product that has faced the most serious safety reckoning of any in this category, and the platform you would sign up for today is substantially more restricted than the one described in older reviews. Both halves belong in an honest assessment.

## What is Character.AI?

Character.AI lets you chat with AI characters — either community-created or your own. Characters have defined personalities, backstories, and speaking styles, and the platform's models are tuned specifically for staying in character and sustaining emotional engagement rather than for reasoning or factual accuracy.

The company runs its own proprietary models. It does not license third-party frontier models, publishes no benchmarks, and offers no public API. Its AvatarFX video generation model is also in-house. This is a deliberately narrow product: it will not help you debug code or analyse a spreadsheet, and it is not trying to.

Leadership changed in June 2025, when Karandeep Anand became CEO, succeeding co-founder Noam Shazeer. Google's 2024 licensing deal, reported at roughly $2.5–3 billion, bought out early investors while leaving the company independent.

## Key Features

- **Millions of community characters** — historical figures, fictional characters, tutors, interviewers, language partners, and original creations.
- **Character creation** — define personality, voice, greeting, and background, then share publicly or keep private.
- **Group chat** — up to ten people alongside up to ten characters in a single conversation.
- **Voice calls** — spoken conversation with characters using distinct voices.
- **Auto Memories** — characters retain details across conversations, so relationships develop continuity.
- **Scenes** — pre-built interactive settings that give a conversation structure rather than starting from a blank prompt.
- **Streams and Community Feed** — social features for sharing conversations and discovering characters.
- **c.ai Series** — AI-generated short-form dramas, part of the platform's move toward entertainment.

## Pros and Cons

**What it does better than anyone**

Character consistency is the genuine technical achievement. Characters hold their voice over long conversations in a way that general assistants prompted to roleplay simply do not. For low-stakes conversational practice — rehearsing a difficult conversation, practising a language, interview prep — it is effective and unintimidating. The free tier is unusually generous, with unlimited messaging, and there is only one paid tier at a modest price rather than a confusing ladder. The creative community is large and genuinely inventive.

**The serious caveats**

Safety is not a footnote here; it is central. In October 2025 the company announced that under-18 users would lose access to open-ended chat entirely, effective by late November 2025, redirected instead to Scenes and Stories, with age assurance to prevent minors opening adult accounts. Mandatory face-scan age verification was reported in 2026. The platform minimum age remains 13.

In January 2026, Character.AI and Google agreed to settle all five family lawsuits — filed in Florida, Texas, Colorado, and New York — on confidential terms with no admission of wrongdoing. These included *Garcia v. Character Technologies*, brought over the death of teenager Sewell Setzer III. In May 2025 Judge Conway had declined to dismiss that case, treating the app as a product and rejecting First Amendment and Section 230 defences; the settlement avoided an appellate ruling on that question.

Regulators have moved too. The FTC issued Section 6(b) orders to seven AI-companion companies in September 2025 regarding minor-safety testing. Italy's data protection authority fined Character Technologies €158,000 in July 2026 for GDPR failures including inadequate transparency and ineffective age verification. California's SB 243, effective January 2026, now regulates companion chatbots directly, requiring AI disclosure to known minors, break reminders, and crisis protocols. Pennsylvania sued in 2026 over a bot allegedly practising medicine without a licence.

There have been implementation stumbles as well: a read-only "reading mode" lockout introduced in early 2026 was withdrawn for adult accounts after adults were wrongly flagged as minors.

Practically, that means: this platform is not appropriate for under-18s in its open-chat form and no longer offers it to them, characters are not qualified to give medical, legal, or mental health advice regardless of how convincingly they do so, and anyone relying on an AI companion for emotional support during a difficult period is better served by talking to a real person or a professional.

## How to Use Character.AI

1. **Sign up at character.ai** on the web or in the mobile apps. Expect age verification.
2. **Browse before creating.** With 16 million characters available, the one you want probably exists already.
3. **Set the scene in your first message.** Establishing context and tone early shapes the whole conversation more than anything you do later.
4. **Use Scenes for structure** if open-ended chat feels aimless — they provide a premise to work from.
5. **Try voice calls** for language practice, where hearing and responding aloud is the point.
6. **Create a character** with a specific purpose — a patient tutor, a tough interviewer — rather than a vague personality.
7. **Keep the frame straight.** Characters are generated text, not people, and not sources of professional advice. That distinction is easy to lose during a long, absorbing conversation, which is precisely why it is worth holding onto.

### FAQs

**Is Character.AI safe for teenagers?**
The company itself decided it was not, in its open-chat form. Since November 2025, under-18 accounts cannot access open-ended chat and are directed to Scenes and Stories instead, with age assurance intended to stop minors creating adult accounts. This followed multiple lawsuits over teen harm, all settled in January 2026, and coincided with new regulation including California's SB 243. Parents should know the platform's minimum age is 13 and that age verification, while now mandatory, is not infallible.

**How much does c.ai+ cost?**
There is one paid tier, c.ai+, at $9.99/month or $94.99/year. Promotional pricing has appeared, including a flash sale offering a first year at $59.99 renewing at $94.99. The free tier includes unlimited messaging, so paying is mainly about faster responses and priority access rather than unlocking core functionality.

**Can Character.AI remember our previous conversations?**
Yes, through Auto Memories, characters retain details across sessions, which is what makes long-running interactions feel continuous. Memory is imperfect and characters do sometimes contradict established details.

**Is Character.AI good for productivity or research?**
No. Its models are tuned for character consistency and emotional engagement, not reasoning or factual accuracy, and the company publishes no benchmarks. For work, research, writing, or coding, a general assistant is the right tool. Character.AI is for conversation and creativity.
