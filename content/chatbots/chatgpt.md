# ChatGPT Explained: What It Does Well, What It Costs, and Where It Struggles

ChatGPT is the product that made conversational AI a household habit rather than a research demo. Four years after launch it is no longer just a chat box — it browses, runs code, reads your files, drafts documents, and in agent mode will click through a website on your behalf. That expansion is exactly why a fresh look is useful: most advice written about ChatGPT is about a version that no longer exists.

This guide covers what the product does in August 2026, what each plan realistically gets you, and the specific weaknesses worth knowing before you build a workflow around it.

## What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a conversational AI assistant built by OpenAI. You type a request in plain language and it responds with text, images, code, or a completed task. Underneath it runs OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family, and the model you get depends on your plan: free and low-cost accounts default to **GPT-5.6 Luna**, while paid accounts get **GPT-5.6 Sol**, which exposes a reasoning slider so you can trade speed for deeper thinking on hard problems. A dedicated Sol Pro tier serves the heaviest technical workloads.

The important shift is that ChatGPT is now a work surface, not just a text generator. It holds memory across conversations, connects to third-party apps, keeps long-running Projects with their own files and instructions, and can be handed a multi-step job to complete while you do something else. Older models including GPT-4o, GPT-4.1 and the original GPT-5 were retired in February 2026, so tutorials referencing them are out of date.

## Key Features

- **Agent mode** — hands ChatGPT a goal rather than a question. It browses, fills forms, and completes multi-step tasks, reporting back with what it did.
- **Deep research** — produces long, cited reports by reading dozens of sources over several minutes. Genuinely useful for market scans and literature reviews.
- **Reasoning control** — on Sol you choose how hard the model thinks. Free users get a "Think" button that grants Luna extra reasoning time on a single reply.
- **Projects and memory** — group related chats with shared files and standing instructions, and let the assistant remember your preferences between sessions.
- **Connectors and apps** — pull context from tools you already use, so answers reference your actual documents instead of generic examples.
- **Codex** — a developer-focused coding environment for writing, reviewing, and refactoring real repositories.
- **Voice and vision** — spoken conversation and image understanding, so you can point a camera at a problem and talk it through.
- **Everywhere availability** — web, iOS, Android, macOS and Windows desktop apps, plus a separately billed developer API.

## Pros and Cons

**What works well**

The free tier became dramatically more generous in August 2026, with the daily cap on text conversations removed. For casual use that alone makes ChatGPT hard to beat on value. The ecosystem is the deepest of any assistant — more integrations, more tutorials, more third-party tooling — so whatever you are trying to do, someone has documented it. Agent mode and deep research are mature rather than experimental, and the desktop apps make it fast enough to sit inside daily work instead of being a destination you visit.

**What to weigh carefully**

OpenAI began showing ads inside ChatGPT in February 2026, including to some paying users, which changed the feel of the product for a lot of people. Model retirements are abrupt: the removal of GPT-4o affected an estimated 800,000 weekly users who preferred its conversational style, and there is no guarantee the model you standardise on today survives the year. Free accounts can silently fall back to a smaller model when tool or upload limits are hit, which makes quality inconsistent in ways that are not always visible. And like every large language model, it still states wrong things confidently — anything factual, legal, medical, or financial needs verification before you act on it.

## How to Use ChatGPT

1. **Create an account** at chatgpt.com, or install the mobile or desktop app. You can start free with no payment details.
2. **Write a specific first prompt.** Include the role you want it to take, the audience, the format, and the length. "Write a 400-word plain-English explainer on tax residency for freelance designers" beats "explain tax residency."
3. **Give it your source material.** Upload the document, paste the data, or connect the relevant app. Grounded answers are far more accurate than answers from memory.
4. **Choose the right effort level.** Use a fast default for drafting and rewriting; switch on deeper reasoning or the Think button for analysis, maths, and debugging.
5. **Iterate instead of restarting.** Reply with what was wrong — "too formal, cut the intro, keep the second example" — rather than rewriting your prompt from scratch.
6. **Set up a Project** for anything ongoing. Standing instructions and shared files stop you re-explaining context every session.
7. **Verify before you ship.** Ask for sources on factual claims, then check them yourself. Treat the output as a capable first draft.

### FAQs

**Is ChatGPT free to use?**
Yes. The free plan costs nothing and, since August 2026, no longer caps how many text messages you can send. Limits still apply to image generation, file uploads, voice, and tool use, and paid plans unlock the stronger Sol models. Paid access starts at $8/month for the Go plan, with Plus at $20/month, higher-usage Pro tiers above that, and per-seat Business and Enterprise plans for organisations. Because OpenAI adjusts these regularly, confirm current figures on the official pricing page.

**Is ChatGPT better than Claude or Gemini?**
It depends on the job. ChatGPT has the widest feature set and the strongest ecosystem, Claude is often preferred for long-document work and writing quality, and Gemini wins when you live inside Google Workspace. If you only pay for one, match it to where your files and your daily work already sit.

**Is it safe to put confidential information into ChatGPT?**
Treat consumer plans as unsuitable for genuinely sensitive material — client data, credentials, unreleased financials, or anything covered by a confidentiality agreement. Business and Enterprise plans offer stronger data handling commitments, and OpenAI provides controls to exclude your conversations from model training. Check your organisation's policy before uploading anything you would not email externally.

**Can ChatGPT access the internet?**
Yes. It searches the web when a question needs current information, and agent mode goes further by navigating pages and taking actions. It will still occasionally summarise a source inaccurately, so click through on anything that matters.
