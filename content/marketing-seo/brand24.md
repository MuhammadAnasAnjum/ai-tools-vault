# Brand24 Review 2026: AI Social Listening Put to the Test

If your brand is being talked about online, the practical problem is simply keeping up. Brand24 is an AI-powered social listening and media monitoring tool built to solve exactly that: it scans the web in real time, collects mentions of your brand, product or campaign, and tells you not just how often you are being discussed but how people feel about it.

Founded in Poland in 2011 and now listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange, Brand24 has grown from a straightforward mention tracker into a broader analytics suite with sentiment analysis, anomaly alerts and, more recently, tools that track how your brand appears inside AI chatbot answers. This review looks at what it does well, where it is weaker, and whether the pricing makes sense for your situation.

## What is Brand24?

Brand24 is a real-time media monitoring platform. It reportedly scans more than 25 million online sources — social networks, news sites, blogs, forums, podcasts and reviews — and surfaces every public mention of the keywords you track. Instead of manually searching for your company name, you set up a project and let the tool collect mentions into a single dashboard, complete with reach, engagement and sentiment data.

Its headline feature is AI sentiment analysis, which classifies mentions as positive, negative or neutral across more than 100 languages and adds emotion analysis on top. Layered over this are spike and anomaly alerts that flag sudden changes in mention volume, an AI Insights feature that summarises trends, and an AI Brand Assistant you can query in plain language. Brand24 is also reported in public summaries to sit within a group associated with Prowly and Semrush, though that connection is best treated as reported rather than confirmed.

A newer addition, Chatbeat, tracks how visible your brand is inside AI and large-language-model answers — an early move into what marketers call answer-engine optimisation, or AEO. As buyers increasingly ask chatbots for recommendations, knowing whether you appear in those answers is becoming genuinely useful.

## Key Features

- **Real-time monitoring** — scans 25M+ sources for public mentions as they happen.
- **AI sentiment analysis** — tags mentions positive, negative or neutral, with emotion detection.
- **Multilingual coverage** — analyses mentions across more than 100 languages.
- **Reach and engagement metrics** — estimates how far mentions travel and how much interaction they attract.
- **Anomaly alerts** — notifies you of sudden spikes or drops in mention volume.
- **AI Insights and Brand Assistant** — summarises trends and answers plain-language questions about your data.
- **Chatbeat (AEO)** — tracks brand visibility inside AI chatbot and LLM answers.
- **Influencer and hashtag tracking** — identifies the accounts and tags driving conversation.

## Pros and Cons

**What works well**

Brand24's core strength is breadth and speed. Casting a net across 25 million sources in real time means you rarely miss a mention, and the dashboard makes it easy to see reach, engagement and sentiment at a glance. The anomaly alerts are genuinely valuable for reputation management — being told the moment mention volume spikes lets you get ahead of a brewing issue rather than discovering it days later. The AI Insights and Brand Assistant lower the analytical barrier for smaller teams, and Chatbeat is a forward-looking bet on AI-answer visibility that few competitors match yet. It is also EU-hosted and positioned as GDPR-compliant, processing only public data.

**Where it falls short**

No sentiment engine is perfect, and Brand24's is no exception. Roughly one in five negative mentions can be misclassified, and sarcasm in particular tends to slip through, so treat any single sentiment tag as directional rather than definitive — the value is in the trend, not the individual label. Coverage of LinkedIn is limited by that platform's API restrictions, which is a real blind spot for B2B brands whose conversations happen there. And there is no free plan, only a 14-day trial, so evaluation time is short.

## Pricing

Brand24 offers a 14-day free trial and no free plan. Reported tiers, billed annually, start with an Individual plan at around $199 per month covering a few keywords, roughly 2,000 mentions and a single user, rising through Team at about $349 per month, then a Pro tier where the more advanced AI features are gated, and a custom-priced Enterprise plan. Crucially, AI is bundled by tier rather than metered by credits — basic sentiment is available on all plans, while advanced AI sits higher up. These figures are second-hand, so verify the current structure on the vendor page before committing.

## How to Use Brand24

1. **Start the free trial** — sign up for the 14-day trial to test the platform against your own brand.
2. **Create a project** — set up a monitoring project for your company, product or campaign.
3. **Add keywords** — enter the brand names, hashtags and phrases you want to track, plus terms to exclude.
4. **Review the mention feed** — watch mentions populate in real time and filter by source or sentiment.
5. **Check sentiment trends** — read sentiment and emotion analysis as directional trends, not absolute verdicts.
6. **Set up alerts** — configure anomaly alerts so you are notified of sudden spikes or drops.
7. **Query the AI assistant** — ask the Brand Assistant plain-language questions and review AI Insights summaries.
8. **Report and act** — export reports for stakeholders and respond to the mentions that matter most.

### FAQs

**Does Brand24 have a free plan?**
A. No. Brand24 offers a 14-day free trial but no permanently free plan. You will need to choose a paid tier to continue after the trial period ends.

**How accurate is Brand24's sentiment analysis?**
A. It is useful but imperfect. Around one in five negative mentions can be misclassified and sarcasm is often missed, so treat sentiment tags as directional signals and review important mentions manually.

**Does Brand24 monitor LinkedIn?**
A. Coverage is limited because of LinkedIn's API restrictions. For heavily B2B brands whose conversations centre on LinkedIn, this is a meaningful blind spot worth factoring into your decision.

**What is Chatbeat?**
A. Chatbeat is a newer Brand24 feature that tracks how visible your brand is inside AI and LLM answers — part of answer-engine optimisation. It helps you see whether chatbots mention you when users ask for recommendations.
