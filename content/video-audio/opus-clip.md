# Opus Clip Review 2026: Turning Long Videos Into Shorts

If you produce long-form video or podcasts and want a steady stream of short vertical clips without editing each one by hand, Opus Clip is probably already on your radar. This Opus Clip review examines what the market leader in long-to-short repurposing does well in 2026, and where its credit model and other quirks may test your patience.

Opus Clip (opus.pro) has grown into one of the most widely used tools in its niche, with a user base reported to exceed ten million and backing from SoftBank's Vision Fund 2 at a valuation said to be around $215 million. That scale matters, because repurposing long content into shorts is now a crowded field, and Opus Clip's head start shows in the polish of its core features.

## What is Opus Clip?

Opus Clip is an AI video-editing tool that takes a long video — a talk, an interview, a stream or a podcast — and automatically finds and cuts the moments most likely to work as short, shareable clips. Instead of scrubbing through an hour of footage yourself, you let the system identify highlights, reframe them for vertical viewing, and add captions.

Its signature feature is "ClipAnything", which lets you prompt the tool to find specific kinds of moments rather than relying purely on automatic selection. That flexibility, combined with virality scoring and automatic reframing, is what has kept Opus Clip near the front of the pack. It is aimed squarely at creators and marketing teams who need volume: many clips from a single source, produced quickly and captioned ready for social platforms.

## Key Features
- **ClipAnything** — Prompt-based clipping that surfaces any type of moment you describe from a long video.
- **Auto-captions** — Automatically generated, styled captions to boost accessibility and retention.
- **Virality scoring** — Each clip receives a score estimating its potential to perform on social feeds.
- **Auto-reframe** — Reframes landscape footage to vertical while keeping the subject centred.
- **B-roll** — Adds supporting footage to make clips feel more produced.
- **Brand templates** — Reusable styling so clips stay consistent with your visual identity.
- **Credit-based processing** — Roughly one credit is used per minute of uploaded video.

## Pros and Cons

**What works well**
For its core job, Opus Clip is genuinely strong. ClipAnything gives you a level of control that pure automation lacks, letting you steer the tool towards the moments you actually care about. The auto-captioning is quick and tidy, the reframing keeps subjects sensibly framed, and the virality scores, while not gospel, offer a useful starting point for deciding what to post first. For a busy creator turning one podcast into a week of social content, the time savings are substantial. The B-roll and brand-template features add a layer of polish that would otherwise take manual editing, and because the whole pipeline runs in the browser there is nothing to install before you start. Teams managing several channels at once tend to get the most out of it, since the consistency the templates enforce keeps every clip on-brand without extra effort.

**Where it falls short**
The credit model draws the most criticism. Consumption can feel steep, and some users report friction when trying to cancel a subscription. There is also a subtler issue with how the AI selects clips: it reportedly discards a large share of the source footage — on the order of forty per cent — when deciding what is worth keeping, which means you may get fewer usable minutes out than you put in. None of this makes the tool a poor choice, but it does mean you should plan your credit budget and read the cancellation terms before committing.

**A note on expectations**
Automatic clip selection is a guide, not a guarantee. The virality score and the AI's choices reflect patterns, not certainties, so a human review pass before publishing remains worthwhile.

**What makes Opus Clip different from rivals?**
Its ClipAnything feature and mature virality scoring, backed by a very large user base, keep it ahead in the long-to-short repurposing niche, though competition is intensifying.

**How does the credit system work?**
Processing costs roughly one credit per minute of uploaded video. Because heavy use can add up, it is worth estimating your monthly volume before choosing a plan.

**How much does Opus Clip cost?**

**Will I get a clip from every part of my video?**
