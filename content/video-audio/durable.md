# Durable AI Review: A Business Website in Thirty Seconds, Not a Media Studio

Before anything else, a plain statement: Durable is not a video or audio tool. It generates no clips, no voiceovers, and no soundtracks. It is an AI website builder, and it sits in this video and audio category for a single, honest reason — it can be the place you publish and host a site that showcases the media you have made elsewhere. If you are hunting for something that creates audio or video, look past this one; if you have that content and need somewhere to put it in front of customers fast, Durable is worth a look.

With that cleared up, the actual pitch is genuinely striking. Durable claims to build a working small-business website in about thirty seconds from a handful of prompts, then bolts on the practical machinery a solo operator needs to run a business: a lightweight CRM, invoicing, and AI marketing agents. This review covers what that speed buys you and where it stops, while being careful never to imply media-generation features the product simply does not have.

## What is Durable?

Durable is an AI-powered website builder aimed at solopreneurs and small businesses. You answer a few short prompts about what you do, and it assembles a complete starter website — layout, copy, and imagery — in around thirty seconds. The idea is to collapse the usual days of fiddling with a site builder into a first draft you can edit, so getting online stops being the thing you keep putting off.

It does not stop at the website. Durable positions itself as a small-business toolkit, adding a simple CRM to track contacts and leads, invoicing to handle getting paid, and AI marketing agents to help with promotion. One clarification worth noting: the correct domain is durable.com, not the .co variant that sometimes causes confusion. In short, it is less a creative studio and more an all-in-one starting point for running the online side of a very small business — with the website generator as the headline act.

## Key Features

- **AI website generation** — produces a complete starter site from a few prompts in about thirty seconds, giving you an instant first draft to refine.
- **Editable templates and copy** — the generated layout, text, and images can be adjusted, so the fast draft becomes your own with light editing.
- **Built-in CRM** — a simple contact and lead manager, keeping customer details in the same place as your site.
- **Invoicing** — create and send invoices so a small business can bill clients without a separate accounting tool.
- **AI marketing agents** — assistants aimed at helping with promotion and marketing tasks for owners who have no dedicated team.
- **Hosting and publishing** — hosts the site it builds, giving you a live web address, which is where a media portfolio could live.
- **Fast setup for non-technical users** — the whole flow is built for people with no coding or design background.
- **Small-business focus** — the feature set is scoped tightly to what a solopreneur needs rather than to enterprise complexity.

## Pros and Cons

**What works well**

Speed is the whole point, and Durable delivers it. For a plumber, coach, or freelancer who has been meaning to get online for months, going from a few prompts to a live starter site in under a minute removes the intimidation that usually stalls the job. The bundled CRM and invoicing add real value for the target user: a solo operator can manage contacts and get paid without stitching together three more subscriptions, and the AI marketing agents lend a hand where there is no marketing team. As a place to publish work, it does the hosting job cleanly — so if you have produced video or audio elsewhere, Durable gives you a quick, tidy site to display it on. For the non-technical owner who wants a presence rather than a project, that convenience is the entire appeal.

**Where it falls short**

The limitation to be clear about is the one this review opened with: Durable makes no media. There is no video generation, no audio tooling, nothing that produces the content you might want to showcase — it only gives you a site to host such content once you have created it elsewhere. Read it as a media tool and you will be let down, because it was never designed to be one. On its own terms as a website builder, the trade-offs are the familiar ones for fast, templated generation: less design flexibility and depth than a hand-built site or a heavyweight platform, and a feature set scoped to small businesses rather than complex or high-growth needs. It is a brilliant starting point, not a ceiling.

**A clear note on the category fit**

To be unmistakable: Durable's only connection to video and audio is as a publishing and hosting destination. It is where a finished portfolio can live, not where any of it is made. Anyone shortlisting it here should do so as "the site my media sits on," never as a source of the media itself.

## How to Use Durable

1. **Go to durable.com** — use the .com domain (not .co) and start the builder to see what it generates for your line of work.
2. **Answer the prompts** — describe your business, what you offer, and your location so the AI has enough to work with.

5. **Add your media** — embed or link the video and audio you produced in other tools to turn the site into a showcase.
6. **Set up CRM and invoicing** — bring in contacts and configure invoicing if you want to run those parts of the business here too.
7. **Try the marketing agents** — use the AI marketing features to help promote the site, checking the output before you publish it.
8. **Publish and share** — take the site live on its web address and start pointing customers and prospects to it.
