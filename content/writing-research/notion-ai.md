# Notion AI Review: Useful Inside Your Workspace, Confusing to Price

Notion AI is not a standalone writing tool. It is a layer inside Notion, the workspace app, and that context is the whole point: its value comes from acting on the notes, docs and databases you already keep there, not from being a better blank-page writer than a dedicated assistant.

That also makes it the tool in this category whose pricing changed most confusingly in the last year. The simple $10-a-month add-on people remember is gone, replaced by something harder to summarise. If you are evaluating Notion AI in 2026, the pricing section matters as much as the features.

## What is Notion AI?

Notion AI is the set of AI features built into the Notion workspace. It can draft and edit text in any document, summarise long pages, turn rough notes into structured content, fill and analyse database entries, and — most usefully — answer questions using the content of your own workspace as context.

That last capability, often called workspace search or Q&A, is what separates it from a general chatbot. Because it can read across your connected pages and databases, it answers questions grounded in your own material rather than the open web or model memory. More recent versions extend this with connectors to other work apps and access to multiple underlying models.

The trade-off is inherent: Notion AI is only as useful as the workspace it sits in. If your team lives in Notion, it is compelling. If you keep little there, most of its advantage disappears.

## Key Features

- **In-line writing and editing** inside any Notion page, from drafting to rewriting to tone changes.
- **Workspace Q&A** that answers using your own connected pages and databases.
- **Summarisation** of long documents and meeting notes.
- **Database automation** — generating, filling and analysing database properties with AI.
- **Connectors** to other work tools on higher tiers, extending Q&A beyond Notion itself.
- **Access to multiple underlying models** rather than a single fixed one.
- **A limited free allowance** so you can try it before paying.

## Pros and Cons

**What works well**

The integration is the entire case for Notion AI, and it is a strong one. Editing a document, summarising a page or querying your workspace without leaving the app removes friction that standalone tools cannot, because they never have your context. For teams whose knowledge already lives in Notion, workspace Q&A turns a pile of scattered docs into something you can actually interrogate.

It is also a low-commitment way into AI-assisted work. If you already pay for Notion, adding its AI is a small step rather than a new tool to learn.

**Where it falls short**

As a pure writer it is unremarkable. For originating long-form content from nothing, a dedicated assistant is at least as good and usually cheaper, so Notion AI earns its place through context, not raw writing quality. Its usefulness is also strictly bounded by how much you keep in Notion — thin workspace, thin value.

And then there is pricing, which is now the most common source of confusion.

**The pricing change, explained**

For a long time Notion AI was a flat $10-per-member add-on on top of any plan. Notion discontinued that standalone add-on around May 2025. AI is now bundled into the Business and Enterprise plans rather than sold separately, with additional usage handled through a credit system — additional credits have been reported at roughly $10 per 1,000. Lower-tier and free users get a limited allowance rather than the full feature set.

The practical effect is that "how much is Notion AI" no longer has a single answer. It depends on your Notion plan, your member count and your usage, and the old $10 figure many comparisons still quote no longer describes how it is sold. Because the vendor page could not be reached at the time of writing, treat the specifics here as indicative and confirm them directly.

## How to Use Notion AI

1. **Confirm your Notion plan first**, since AI access and allowances now depend on whether you are on a free, Plus, Business or Enterprise plan.
2. **Put your real material in Notion** before judging the tool — its advantage is context, and it cannot show that on an empty workspace.
3. **Use workspace Q&A as the headline feature**, asking questions your own docs should answer rather than treating it as a general chatbot.
4. **Lean on it for summarising and restructuring** existing content, which is where it beats a blank-page writer.
5. **Watch your credit consumption** under the new metering, especially across a team, so usage does not quietly outrun expectations.
6. **Use a dedicated assistant for heavy long-form drafting** and keep Notion AI for work anchored in your workspace.
7. **Check current pricing on Notion's own page**, because the model changed recently and older figures are misleading.

### FAQs

**Is Notion AI worth it?**
If your team already works in Notion, yes — the workspace Q&A and in-context editing remove real friction that standalone tools cannot. If you keep little in Notion or mainly need blank-page drafting, a dedicated assistant is better value.

**How much does Notion AI cost now?**
The old flat $10-per-member add-on was discontinued around May 2025. AI is now included in Business and Enterprise plans, with extra usage metered by credits (additional credits reported around $10 per 1,000) and a limited allowance on lower tiers. Confirm the current structure on Notion's own pricing page, since it changed recently.

**How is Notion AI different from ChatGPT?**
The key difference is context: Notion AI can read and act on your own connected pages and databases, answering questions grounded in your material. A general chatbot has no access to your workspace. For blank-page writing they are comparable; for querying your own knowledge, Notion AI wins.

**Does Notion AI have a free tier?**
Free and lower-tier plans include a limited AI allowance rather than the full feature set, which is enough to evaluate whether the workspace integration is useful for you before moving to a plan that includes it fully.
