# Writesonic: No Longer an AI Writer, Now an AI Search Visibility Platform

Writesonic sold itself for years as an AI copywriting tool: feed it a topic, get articles, ads and product descriptions. It has repositioned around a different and more current problem — whether AI systems mention your brand when people ask them questions.

The pitch now is generative engine optimisation. When someone asks ChatGPT or Gemini for a recommendation in your category, do you get named? Writesonic tracks that, and treats it as the successor to keyword rankings.

Whether the pivot is right is arguable. That it happened is not, and it changes who should be reading a review of this tool.

## What is Writesonic?

Writesonic is a platform for measuring and improving how visible a brand is inside AI-generated answers. Its **AI Visibility Tracker** monitors mentions and citations across systems including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews, sliced by competitor, market, language, topic, intent and date.

Around that sit autonomous agents for content production, outreach and technical SEO work, plus an MCP integration letting it connect to tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor and n8n.

The legacy writing product still exists. **AI Article Writer** continues to ship, now in a sixth version. It is no longer the headline.

The wider family confuses people, so to be clear about what is what: **Chatsonic** is the conversational assistant with live web search, file uploads and multi-model access. **Botsonic** builds custom chatbots trained on your own site and documentation. **Photosonic** handles image generation and **Audiosonic** covers audio. These are best understood as related products that may be billed separately rather than as one bundle, so check what your subscription actually includes.

The company is US-based and Y Combinator-backed.

## Key Features

- **AI visibility tracking** across the major AI answer engines, the current flagship capability.
- **Competitor comparison** showing who gets recommended instead of you.
- **Autonomous SEO agents** for content, outreach and technical fixes.
- **AI Article Writer** for long-form drafting, the surviving legacy product.
- **Chatsonic** for conversational research with real-time web access.
- **Botsonic** for building support chatbots on your own content.
- **MCP integration** connecting Writesonic to other AI tools and automation platforms.
- **Multi-model access** on higher tiers rather than a single provider.

## Pros and Cons

**What makes the pivot interesting**

The underlying problem is real and getting more so. A meaningful share of buying research now happens inside AI assistants that produce one answer rather than ten links, and being absent from that answer is a commercial problem no traditional rank tracker measures. Tooling in this space is young, and Writesonic moved earlier than most.

Aggregate ratings are strong — around 4.7 on both G2 and Trustpilot across thousands of reviews — which is unusually consistent for this category, where platforms often disagree sharply.

**What to be careful about**

Pricing is the weakest part of this review and I would rather say so than invent precision. Reported figures do not reconcile: entry tiers appear around $79 monthly, a mid tier near $100, and a professional tier quoted at both $249 and $199 depending on billing, while other comparisons cite a $16 to $79 range for the writing-focused product. That spread almost certainly reflects legacy writing plans and newer visibility plans being quoted side by side. Reviews also note pricing "changes often and has been re-tiered multiple times." **Treat any price table you find, including any figure in this paragraph, as needing confirmation at checkout.**

Metering has moved to **credits** rather than word counts, and credit and word limits are both recurring complaints. Interface clutter is another consistent theme, which is a predictable consequence of a product family that grew this many branches.

Refunds are narrow: a Better Business Bureau complaint records a refund refused under a **seven-day-from-purchase** policy, with a downgrade or discount offered instead. Eligibility varies by subscription type and duration, so read the terms before an annual commitment.

There is also a strategic risk worth naming. Generative engine optimisation is a young discipline with no settled methodology, and measurements depend on prompting AI systems whose behaviour changes without notice. The category may prove durable, but the current metrics are less stable than search rankings, and anyone buying should expect the methodology to keep moving.

## How to Use Writesonic

1. **Work out which product you want.** Visibility tracking, article writing and chatbot building are different jobs, and the pricing you are quoted depends on which one you are buying.
2. **Start with the free entry point** to see the interface before committing, since the limits are not clearly documented and are best discovered directly.
3. **Baseline your visibility first.** Run the tracker before changing anything, so you have something to compare against later.
4. **Check competitors, not just yourself.** Knowing who gets recommended in your place is more actionable than your own score.
5. **Treat the visibility data as directional.** AI answers vary between runs and across users, so read trends over weeks rather than reacting to single results.
6. **Confirm exactly what your plan includes** regarding Chatsonic, Botsonic and Photosonic, since these may bill separately and stacked subscriptions are easy to accumulate accidentally.
7. **Choose monthly billing initially**, given the seven-day refund window and how often the tiers have been restructured.

### FAQs

**Is Writesonic still an AI writing tool?**
Partly. AI Article Writer still ships and is now in a sixth version, but the company has repositioned around AI search visibility and generative engine optimisation. If all you want is help drafting articles, you are buying a secondary feature of a platform aimed at a different problem, and cheaper dedicated options exist.

**Have Chatsonic and Botsonic been discontinued?**
No, both are active. Chatsonic is the conversational assistant with real-time web search and multi-model support; Botsonic builds chatbots trained on your own content. Photosonic and Audiosonic also still appear in the suite. Confirm which are included in your plan rather than assuming a bundle, since some are sold separately.

**How much does Writesonic cost?**
Reported figures conflict substantially, with entry plans around $79 monthly and professional tiers quoted between roughly $199 and $249, alongside lower figures that appear to describe legacy writing plans. Pricing has been re-tiered repeatedly and is now credit-based. Check the official pricing page — this is a tool where third-party price tables are unusually unreliable.

**What is generative engine optimisation?**
It is the practice of improving how often and how favourably AI systems mention your brand when answering relevant questions, as opposed to improving your position in a list of search results. It matters because AI assistants increasingly return a single synthesised answer, so being omitted means being invisible regardless of your search rankings.
