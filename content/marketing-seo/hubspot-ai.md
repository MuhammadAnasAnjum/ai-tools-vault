# HubSpot AI Review 2026: Is Breeze Worth It in Your CRM?

HubSpot spent the last two years folding its scattered AI features into a single brand called Breeze, and the result is one of the more coherent AI offerings in the CRM world. If you already run your marketing, sales and service on HubSpot, Breeze is designed to sit inside those tools and act on the data you already hold — drafting emails, summarising records and, increasingly, doing multi-step work on your behalf.

That tight integration is both the appeal and the catch. Breeze is genuinely useful when your customer data lives in HubSpot, and its entry-level assistant is free on every edition. But the pricing beyond that gets complicated, and a data-governance controversy in mid-2026 is worth understanding before you lean on it. This review breaks down the three parts of Breeze, the honest trade-offs and how to get started.

## What is HubSpot AI (Breeze)?

Breeze is HubSpot's umbrella brand for artificial intelligence, introduced at its INBOUND 2024 conference. It replaced a patchwork of older names — the ChatSpot chatbot and "Breeze Copilot" were both retired at INBOUND 2025, and the in-product assistant is now simply "Breeze Assistant." Understanding the branding matters, because HubSpot's own documentation and third-party articles still use the old terms in places.

Breeze is organised into three pillars. Breeze Assistant is the in-product AI helper that drafts and refines content, understands the page you are on and the CRM data behind it, and can update records for you. Breeze Agents are more autonomous — think Customer Agent, Prospecting Agent and Data Agent — built and configured in a workspace called Breeze studio to carry out multi-step tasks with less supervision.

The third pillar, Breeze Intelligence, handles data enrichment and buyer-intent signals. It is the rebranded version of Clearbit, the enrichment company HubSpot acquired in late 2023, now folded into the platform as a credit-based add-on that layers extra context onto your contact records.

## Key Features

- **Breeze Assistant** — in-product AI that drafts, refines and summarises using your CRM context. Free on all editions.
- **Breeze Agents** — autonomous agents for customer service, prospecting and data tasks.
- **Breeze studio** — the workspace where you build and configure those agents.
- **Breeze Intelligence** — data enrichment and buyer-intent scoring, the rebranded Clearbit.
- **CRM-aware drafting** — content generation grounded in your actual contacts, deals and tickets.
- **Record updates** — the assistant can edit CRM records, not just suggest text.
- **Unified HubSpot Credits** — a single credit system meters enrichment and some agent usage.
- **Outcome-based agent pricing** — some agents are reportedly billed per result, such as per resolved conversation.

## Pros and Cons

**What works well**

Breeze's advantage is context. Because the assistant understands the CRM record or page you are looking at, its drafts and summaries are grounded in your actual data rather than generic prompts — that is the whole point, and it is where Breeze beats standalone AI writers for HubSpot users. The barrier to entry is also refreshingly low: Breeze Assistant is included free on every edition, including the free CRM, so you can try it without a new purchase. For teams already committed to HubSpot, the agents and enrichment extend naturally from tools they already know.

**Where it falls short**

The value is bounded by how much you actually keep in HubSpot. If your data is scattered across other systems, Breeze has less context to work with and the appeal shrinks. Pricing is the other friction point: between HubSpot Credits, credit-pack add-ons for Breeze Intelligence and outcome-based billing for some agents, working out what you will actually pay is genuinely complex and can climb quickly at scale. Budget carefully and model your expected usage before committing.

**The July 2026 data controversy**

Anyone weighing Breeze should know about a mid-2026 episode. On 1 July 2026, HubSpot updated its terms to enrol customers by default into a shared data-enrichment programme — feeding a planned "Contact Discovery" feature — alongside default-on intent signals and AI training on sent emails. Critics said opting out required toggling several settings and worried that enrichment could supplement other customers' records. After roughly four days of customer revolt, HubSpot reversed course on 5 July 2026 and apologised, its chief product and technology officer stating, "We made a mistake… We will not move forward with the terms of service changes." No customer data was actually pooled, as the feature never activated. It is fair to read this as a proposed default-on change that was withdrawn after backlash rather than an ongoing practice — but it is useful context on how the company approaches data governance.

## How to Use HubSpot AI (Breeze)

1. **Enable Breeze Assistant** — it is included on all editions; switch it on inside your HubSpot account.
2. **Review your data settings** — check enrichment, intent and AI-training toggles so you control what is shared.
3. **Draft with context** — open a record or page and ask the assistant to draft or refine relevant content.
4. **Let it update records** — where useful, allow the assistant to edit CRM records rather than copying text manually.
5. **Explore Breeze studio** — if you need automation, build an agent such as a Customer or Prospecting Agent.
6. **Add Breeze Intelligence** — for enrichment, buy a credit pack on top of your paid subscription and enrich contacts.
7. **Track credit usage** — monitor HubSpot Credits and outcome-based agent charges so costs stay predictable.
8. **Verify pricing** — confirm current credit rates and add-on prices on HubSpot's page before scaling up.

### FAQs

**Is HubSpot's AI free?**
A. Breeze Assistant is included free on every HubSpot edition, including the free CRM. However, Breeze Intelligence enrichment and some agents are metered separately by credits on top of a paid subscription.

**What happened to ChatSpot and Breeze Copilot?**
A. Both names were retired. HubSpot consolidated its AI under the Breeze brand, and the in-product assistant is now called Breeze Assistant following changes announced at INBOUND 2025.

**What is Breeze Intelligence?**
A. It is HubSpot's data-enrichment and buyer-intent product, the rebranded version of Clearbit, which HubSpot acquired in late 2023. It adds context to contact records via a credit-based add-on.

**Should the July 2026 data episode worry me?**
A. It is worth knowing. HubSpot proposed a default-on data-sharing change, faced backlash and reversed it within days; no data was pooled. Treat it as context on data governance rather than an active practice.
